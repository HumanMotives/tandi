# Bandprofiel HUMAN/MOTIVES
# Claude leest dit bij elke batch om bronnen en zinnen te kiezen. Gewone taal, Engels of Nederlands.
# Pas gerust aan: hoe concreter, hoe beter de keuzes.

HUMAN/MOTIVES is an electronic music project: ambient, generative, procedural, minimalist.
Influences: Gijs Gieskes, Jan Jelinek, Eliane Radigue.

We use spoken-word samples from old horror and science-fiction films, old-time radio plays and
broadcasts, and real interviews about strange experiences: alien abduction, UFO sightings,
contactees, missing time, the unexplained.

What makes a line work for us:
- It is eerie, uncanny, dreamlike or strangely beautiful, and stands on its own without the story.
- Calm, sincere or bewildered voices often work better than shouting or melodrama.
- Short statements about seeing, hearing or feeling something that should not be there.
- Lines about machines, signals, space, time, the mind, being watched or being taken.
- The texture of old recordings is welcome: tape, radio static, room sound.

Not wanted: jokes, plot exposition, character names, announcers and commercials,
audiobook narration, modern podcasts.
